# Product Positioning

Status: Draft v0.1 (Playbook step L1) · Date: 2026-09-24
Everything here is a **hypothesis until the interviews confirm it** (see `interview-guide.md`).
No market statistics are quoted, because we have not researched any. Where a number would help, a `TODO(research)` marks the gap.

## 1. Category

**What we are:** a guided budgeting and money-organisation tool, the digital version of The Ledger Loft planners.

**What we are not:** a financial adviser, a debt counsellor, a credit bureau, a bank or an investment platform.

We help people **see, plan and reflect** on their own numbers. We don't tell them what to buy, invest in or sign up for.

## 2. Target user

### Primary (MVP): "The planner who wants it to add up for them"

- ASSUMPTION: an adult in South Africa who has already bought, or would buy, a printable or digital budget planner.
- Earns a regular income (salaried, commission, or a mix with a side hustle) and is paid monthly, often on or around the 25th.
- Already *wants* to budget and has tried to: paper planners, spreadsheets, notes apps.
- Main frustration: the maths and the upkeep, not the motivation. Totals drift, a missed month means starting over, and a spreadsheet feels cold.
- Cares about privacy. May be uneasy giving an app bank login details.

### Secondary (later, not MVP)

- Couples or households sharing costs (depends on owner answer Q2 in the operating plan).
- Irregular or freelance earners who need flexible budgets.

### Explicitly not targeted

- People in acute financial distress who need debt review or legal help. We **signpost** them to regulated help (see `safety-boundary.md`) rather than try to serve them.
- Investors looking for portfolio tools.

## 3. Core problem (hypothesis)

> "I know I should budget and I like planning, but keeping totals, debts and savings in sync by hand every month
> is tedious and error-prone. When I fall behind I lose the thread and give up until next month."

What the interviews need to test:
1. Is the pain **upkeep and maths** (our hypothesis), or something else such as motivation, knowledge, or partner alignment?
2. Is manual entry acceptable, or is it a deal-breaker?
3. Would they pay monthly, or do they prefer a once-off purchase like the planners?

## 4. Value proposition

**Headline:** Your Ledger Loft planner, with the maths done for you.

**Supporting line:** Plan your month, track what really happened, and watch your goals and debts move, without linking your bank account.

| Pain | What Ledger Loft does | Proof we can honestly show |
|---|---|---|
| Totals don't add up | Automatic planned vs actual and remaining-to-budget | Every figure has a "how this was calculated" view |
| Missed a month, gave up | Monthly check-in that picks up where you left off; no penalty for gaps | Check-in flow in the demo |
| Debts feel overwhelming | Lists debts and shows snowball and avalanche orderings as *estimates* | Clearly labelled estimate screens |
| Saving for irregular costs | Sinking funds for school fees, car licence, December and so on | Sinking fund tracker |
| Privacy worries | No bank login, manual entry only, export and delete your data any time | Settings screen and privacy page |
| Wants something beautiful | Same calm, premium look as the planners, with printable monthly summaries | Export sample |

### Why us rather than a spreadsheet or a generic budgeting app

- **Continuity with a brand they already bought and trust.** Existing planner buyers get a familiar structure.
- **Guided rather than blank.** Setup walks through income, bills, debts and goals in order.
- **Local by default.** Rand, the month-end payday rhythm, and South African cost categories such as school fees, the car licence disc and medical aid.
- **No bank connection**, so there is less to trust us with.

## 5. Trust principles

These are product rules as well as marketing claims. Every feature has to honour them.

1. **Your data, your control.** We collect only what budgeting needs. Export and delete are one click away.
2. **No bank logins.** We never ask for bank credentials.
3. **No selling or sharing of personal financial data** for advertising. ASSUMPTION: the owner commits to this; it goes in the privacy policy.
4. **Show the working.** Every calculated number can be expanded to show its formula and inputs.
5. **Estimates are labelled as estimates.** Payoff dates and projections carry an "Estimate" label and state their assumptions.
6. **No shame.** Negative balances and missed months are described neutrally, with a next step and no judgement.
7. **No advice, no pressure.** No product recommendations, no affiliate credit offers, no "you should" statements about financial products.
8. **Plain language.** No jargon without an explanation. Readable at a general-public level.

## 6. Positioning statement

For South Africans who like to plan their money but are tired of doing the maths and upkeep by hand,
The Ledger Loft is a guided budgeting planner that keeps your monthly plan, debts, goals and sinking funds in sync,
without connecting your bank account. Unlike spreadsheets or generic budgeting apps, it follows the structure and calm
style of The Ledger Loft planners, and it shows how every number is worked out.
