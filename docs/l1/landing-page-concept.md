# Landing Page Concept

Status: Draft v0.1 (Playbook step L1) · Date: 2026-09-24
Purpose: a **pilot waitlist page** to test demand. It is not the final marketing site.
All copy follows the wording rules in `safety-boundary.md`.

## Page structure

### 1. Hero
- **Headline:** Your Ledger Loft planner, with the maths done for you.
- **Subheadline:** Plan your month, track what really happened, and watch your goals and debts move, all without linking your bank account.
- **Primary call to action:** Join the founding pilot
- **Secondary call to action:** See how it works ↓
- **Visual:** a calm dashboard mock-up in planner styling, showing synthetic numbers only.

### 2. "Sound familiar?" (reflects interview language; update after interviews)
- "My totals never quite add up."
- "I miss one month and have to start over."
- "I've got debts in five places and no clear picture."
- "December and school fees always catch me off guard."

### 3. How it works (3 steps)
1. **Set up in one sitting.** A guided walk-through of your income, bills, debts and goals.
2. **Plan each month.** Give every rand a job, or keep it flexible. Your choice.
3. **Check in and reflect.** Record what happened, see your progress, and download a planner-style summary.

### 4. Features
- Monthly budget: planned vs actual, remaining to budget
- Savings goals and sinking funds
- Debt list with snowball and avalanche orderings (labelled as estimates)
- Monthly check-in and reflection prompts
- Printable summary in The Ledger Loft style
- "Show the working" on every number

### 5. Privacy and trust
- No bank logins, ever. You enter what you choose.
- Export or delete your data any time.
- We don't sell your personal financial data.
- Link to the privacy notice. `TODO(legal)`

### 6. Founding pilot offer
Summarise from `pilot-offer.md`: what's included, price, duration, cancel any time, limited places (only if that is actually true).

### 7. FAQ
- **Is this financial advice?** No. It's a budgeting and planning tool. See the disclaimer.
- **Do I need to link my bank?** No. Everything is entered manually.
- **Does it work with my printed planner?** Yes, use them side by side. Monthly summaries can be printed.
- **What happens after the pilot?** You keep your data and choose whether to continue.
- **Can I cancel?** Yes, any time. `TODO(legal)`: confirm wording against refund and cooling-off policy.

Short disclaimer (from `safety-boundary.md`) · Privacy · Terms · Contact · © The Ledger Loft

## What to measure (privacy-light)

| Event | Why |
|---|---|
| Page view (by source: Etsy, Instagram, direct) | Which channel brings interest |
| "Join pilot" clicked | Intent |
| Waitlist form submitted (email only, plus optional "Do you own a Ledger Loft planner?" Y/N) | Demand |
| Pilot payment completed | Real demand |

Use cookie-less or consented analytics. Collect **no** financial data on the landing page.
