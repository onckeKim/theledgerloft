# Founding Pilot Offer

Status: Draft v0.1 (Playbook step L1) · Date: 2026-09-24
**Price set by the owner on 2026-09-24: R 50,00 once-off for 90 days (D-043).** No competitor pricing was researched.

## Purpose

Get a small group of real users to (a) pay something, which proves demand, (b) use the MVP for 2–3 months, and
(c) give structured feedback. Paying pilots give far better signal than free sign-ups.

## Offer shape

| Element | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Name | The Ledger Loft Founding Members |
| Size | ASSUMPTION: 20–30 places. Say "limited" only if the cap is real |
| Duration | 3 months of access to the MVP as it is built |
| Price | **R 50,00 once-off** for 90 days of access (set in `private.plans`; no subscription) |
| Bonus | A free digital Ledger Loft planner of their choice (an existing product, so no extra cost to make) |
| Commitment asked | One 20-minute feedback call per month, plus in-app check-ins |
| Refund | Full refund within the first 14 days, no questions asked. ASSUMPTION: confirm against the ECT Act cooling-off rules (see `safety-boundary.md`) |
| Payment | PayFast once-off payment link (needs no subscription integration). The owner confirms merchant setup |

## What pilots get

- Early access to guided setup, monthly budget, goals, debts, sinking funds and monthly summary export, as each is released.
- A direct line to the founder for feedback.
- A founding price (if the monthly option is chosen).

## What pilots do **not** get

- Financial advice or one-on-one money coaching. That stays out of scope and inside the safety boundary.
- A guaranteed feature list or dates. Be honest that this is early software.

## Pilot success criteria (to agree before launch)

ASSUMPTIONS for the owner to adjust:
- At least 60% of pilots complete guided setup.
- At least 50% complete at least 2 monthly check-ins.
- At least 40% say they would be "very disappointed" if the product went away.
- At least 30% say they would continue at the planned paid price.

## Sequencing

1. Run interviews (`interview-guide.md`) and refine this offer from what people say.
2. Open pilot sign-ups (waitlist and payment) once the onboarding slice (L6) is close to ready. Don't take money for
   software that is months away.
3. Deliver access in the order onboarding (L6), then dashboard (L7), then reports (L8).
