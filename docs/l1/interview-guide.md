# Customer Interview Guide

Status: Draft v0.1 (Playbook step L1) · Date: 2026-09-24
Goal: confirm or reject the core-problem hypothesis in `positioning.md` **before** building.

## Logistics

- **Who:** 5–10 people. Aim for at least 5 past Ledger Loft Etsy buyers and 2–3 people who budget but have never bought.
- **How:** 20–30 minute video or voice call, or WhatsApp voice notes if easier.
- **Recruit:** Etsy buyer message (check Etsy's messaging rules first; see L10), personal network, planner communities.
- **Thank-you:** ASSUMPTION: a free planner download or a founding-member discount. No cash, so nobody feels pressure to be positive.
- **Consent:** ask to take notes. Record only with explicit permission. Store notes without surnames or financial figures (POPIA: minimal data).

### Recruitment message (draft)

> Hi [first name], thank you for buying [planner name] from The Ledger Loft. I'm exploring a simple budgeting app
> based on the planners and would love 20 minutes of your honest opinion. There's nothing to buy. As a thank-you
> I'll send you [free planner]. Would you be open to a quick chat this week?

## Ground rules for the interviewer

- Ask about **past behaviour**, not future intentions ("Tell me about the last time…", not "Would you…").
- Don't pitch the app until the last section.
- **Never ask for actual income, debt amounts or account details.** We need habits, not numbers.
- If someone becomes distressed about money, pause, be kind, and offer the signposting info from `safety-boundary.md`.

## Script

### 1. Warm-up (3 min)
1. How do you currently keep track of your money, if at all?
2. What made you buy a planner? *(buyers)* / What made you start budgeting? *(non-buyers)*

### 2. Current process (8 min)
3. Walk me through the last time you sat down to do your budget. When was it? What did you use?
4. What happened after payday last month? What did you do first?
5. How long does a budgeting session take you?
6. What do you use besides the planner: spreadsheets, apps, notes, banking app?

### 3. Pain (8 min)
7. What's the most annoying part of budgeting for you?
8. Tell me about a time you stopped keeping your budget. What happened?
9. How do you keep track of debts or accounts, if you have them? *(don't ask amounts)*
10. How do you plan for yearly or irregular costs such as school fees, licence renewals or December?
11. Have you tried a budgeting app? What happened? Why did you stop, or keep going?

### 4. Trust and privacy (4 min)
12. How would you feel about an app that asked you to link your bank account? Why?
13. Would typing in amounts yourself be fine, or a deal-breaker?
14. What would make you trust a new money app with your information?

### 5. Value and willingness to pay (5 min)
15. If something solved [their stated pain], what would it be worth to you?
16. How do you usually pay for things like this: once-off, monthly or yearly?
17. *(Now show the landing-page concept or a sketch)* What stands out? What's confusing? What's missing?
18. Would you join a paid pilot at [pilot offer]? What would stop you?

### 6. Close (2 min)
19. Anything I should have asked but didn't?
20. May I contact you when the pilot opens?

## Decision criteria (write these down before interviewing)

Proceed to A2 and L2 if **most** of the following hold. Thresholds are ASSUMPTIONS for the owner to adjust.
- At least 60% describe upkeep, maths or falling behind as a top-3 pain.
- At least 60% say manual entry is acceptable.
- At least 3 people commit to the paid pilot (payment or firm written commitment, not "sounds nice").

If these don't hold, revisit positioning before writing code.
