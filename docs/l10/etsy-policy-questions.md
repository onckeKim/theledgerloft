# Etsy Policy Questions (L10 gate)

Status: **Open.** Date: 2026-09-24. Owner: shop owner (Ledger Loft & Co).

The playbook says not to assume current Etsy policy. Nothing in the funnel that touches Etsy (listings, product
files, inserts, Etsy messages, buyer data) gets built until each question below is answered from **Etsy's own
official pages**, with the page, the quote and the date recorded here.

**Why they're still open:** Etsy's sites (etsy.com, help.etsy.com) are blocked by this project's build environment,
so I couldn't read them. I haven't filled in any answers from memory, because policy changes and memory can be wrong.

**Where to look.** These are the Etsy documents that usually cover these topics. Confirm each one exists and is
current when you check:
- Seller Policy
- House Rules: Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Prohibited Items, Fees and Payments, Intellectual Property
- Messages Policy
- Etsy Help Center articles on digital items and on contacting buyers

## How to record an answer

For each question, fill in: **Answer** (yes / no / with conditions), **Quote** (the exact sentence), **Source** (page
title and URL), **Checked on** (date). If the policy is unclear, write "unclear" and ask Etsy Support in writing. Keep
their reply.
