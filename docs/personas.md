# Personas

Status: v0.1 (Playbook step A2). **These are proto-personas**: assumptions to validate in the L1 interviews, not research
findings. Names are illustrative. There are no demographic statistics.

## P1: Thandi, "the planner who's tired of the maths" (primary)
- **Situation:** salaried, paid monthly around the 25th, plus a small side income. Has bought a Ledger Loft & Co budget planner.
- **Does today:** fills in the planner on payday, sometimes keeps a spreadsheet; falls behind mid-month.
- **Wants:** a plan that adds itself up, a clear view of debts and yearly costs (school fees, car licence, December), the same calm look as the planner.
- **Worries:** giving bank logins to an app; feeling judged when a month goes badly.
- **Success looks like:** finishes setup in one sitting, checks in weekly, completes the monthly review.
- **Design implications:** guided onboarding, "show the working", sinking funds, neutral copy, no bank linking.

## P2: Sam, "the irregular earner" (secondary)
- **Situation:** freelance or commission income that changes month to month.
- **Wants:** a flexible budget that doesn't break when income is lower; a quick way to log income as it arrives.
- **Worries:** zero-based budgets feel impossible with uncertain income.
- **Design implications:** flexible budgeting style (default), "It varies" pay frequency, planning on a typical or lower amount, actual-vs-planned income in reviews.

## P3: Lerato and Jordan, "the household" (later: partner sharing, A-02)
- **Situation:** two adults sharing costs.
- **Wants:** one shared plan, with each person able to record spending.
- **Design implications:** household model and roles are in the schema from L5, but invites and sharing ship after MVP.

## Anti-persona: someone in acute debt distress
- Needs regulated debt counselling or legal help, not a planner.
- We **don't target or market to** this person. If they find us, the app stays useful for organising and **signposts** regulated help (`docs/l1/safety-boundary.md` section 5). It never offers restructuring or negotiation.

## Anti-persona: the investor or optimiser
- Wants portfolio tracking, returns or product comparisons. Out of scope, and advice territory.
